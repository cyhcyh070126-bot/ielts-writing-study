句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The diagram illustrates how [product] is produced / how [natural process] takes place.

制造用is produced；自然循环不用硬套制造。

概述

Overall, the process comprises [main phases], beginning with [start] and ending with [output / return].

循环要说明回到起点，有分支时概括各去向。

步骤衔接

After [stage], [material] is [next action], before being [following action].

加工步骤可用被动语态，自然过程按实际动作选主动。
