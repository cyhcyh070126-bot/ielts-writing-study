展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

汽车占比下降，公交和自行车上升；2030 年汽车预计仍是最大的单项，但与另两项的差距缩小。

  • 细节一比较两个历史年份；细节二单独交代预测，并比较 2010—2030 的总变化。
  • 2010 和 2020 用过去时；2030 用 is projected to、is expected to 等预测表达。

易错点

  • 汽车仍然是最大的单项，但公交加自行车的合计预计更高；两句话可以同时成立。
  • from 60% to 40% 是下降 20 个百分点，不是下降 20%。
  • 预测不等于实际结果，2030 年数据要保留 projected／expected 等标记。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The chart compares the shares of commuters using [modes] in [place] in [past years] and gives projections for [future year].

引言直接区分实测与预测。

概述

Overall, the share travelling by [A] fell and is expected to decline further, while [B] and [C] show the opposite pattern. [A] is nevertheless projected to remain the largest category.

趋势和未来排名须同时与预测数据一致。

细节一

In [year], [A] was the main commuting method for [x]% of commuters, compared with [y]% for [B] and [z]% for [C]. By [year], these figures had changed to [figures].

分母为通勤人数，不是出行次数；commuters 与 journeys 不能互换。

细节二

By [future year], [A] is forecast to represent [x]%, while [B] and [C] are expected to reach [figures]. This would reduce the gap between [A] and [B] to [difference] percentage points.

预测结果不能写成已发生的事实。

完整原创范文

The bar chart compares the proportions of Brookfield commuters whose main means of transport was a car, a bus or a bicycle in 2010 and 2020, and provides forecasts for 2030.

Overall, the proportion using cars declined and is projected to fall further, while both bus travel and cycling are expected to gain a larger share. Cars are nevertheless forecast to remain the most common individual method in 2030.

In 2010, cars accounted for 60% of commuters, considerably more than buses and bicycles, at 25% and 15% respectively. By 2020, the car figure had fallen by ten percentage points to 50%. Over the same decade, the proportions travelling by bus and bicycle each rose by five points, reaching 30% and 20%.

The projections indicate a continuation of these changes. In 2030, 40% of commuters are expected to use cars, compared with 35% using buses and 25% cycling. The gap between cars and buses would therefore narrow from 35 percentage points in 2010 to just five points. Meanwhile, the combined share for buses and bicycles is projected to reach 60%.
