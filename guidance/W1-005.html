展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

West 三项指标均最大，East 均最小，North 居中；主要比较排名一致性，不必寻找不存在的时间趋势。

  • 细节一合写会员与借书量；细节二比较面积并补充面积、借阅量的相对差距。
  • 2025 年数据用一般过去时；不同列的单位不同，不能直接把面积与人数相加比较。

易错点

  • annual book loans 指借阅次数，不是馆藏图书数量。
  • West 与 East 面积之比约为 1.83；范文用 approximately half，不写 exactly half。
  • 静态表格没有前后年份，不要用 rose／fell 描述三个馆的横向差异。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The table compares [places] in terms of [indicator 1], [indicator 2] and [indicator 3] in [year].

in terms of 后用并列名词短语交代指标。

概述

Overall, [A] recorded the highest figures for all three measures, whereas [B] had the lowest. [C] occupied an intermediate position throughout.

仅当所有列排序一致时才使用 all three／throughout。

细节一

[A] had [x] members, compared with [y] at [C] and [z] at [B]. Annual lending followed the same order, at [figures], respectively.

把同一排名的两列放在一起，避免逐行机械抄表。

细节二

In terms of floor area, [A] covered [x] square metres, while [C] and [B] measured [figures]. The difference between the largest and smallest was [difference].

面积要明确单位；补充差距必须先计算。

完整原创范文

The table compares three public libraries, North, East and West, in terms of their membership numbers, annual book loans and total floor areas in 2025.

Overall, West was the largest library on all three measures, while East recorded the lowest figures throughout. North occupied the middle position, with both its membership and lending totals lying between those of the other two libraries.

West had 15,000 members, compared with 12,000 at North and 9,000 at East. Annual lending followed the same ranking. West issued 210,000 book loans during the year, while the figures for North and East were 180,000 and 135,000 respectively. The difference in lending between the largest and smallest libraries was therefore 75,000 loans, alongside a membership gap of 6,000 people.

The floor area of West was 2,200 square metres, which was 400 square metres greater than North's 1,800. East was considerably smaller, at 1,200 square metres, giving it approximately half the floor area of West. Although North had 3,000 more members than East, its additional floor space amounted to 600 square metres.
