展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

美国始终最高，澳大利亚始终最低。美国和澳大利亚中途下降后回到起点，英国在开头持平后一路下行。

  • 引言写三国、人数、留学方向和时段。The graph tracks the number of [people from X] who [activity] between [years].
  • 总览把排名与趋势分开。[A] remained highest throughout. [B] was the only series to finish clearly below its starting level.
  • 主体一比较同向的美国和澳大利亚，分别列起点、低点、恢复后的数值。用 recovered to 和 returned to 区分回升与回到同一点。
  • 主体二单写英国的持平和下降，再用末年差距连接三条线。以 roughly、about、approximately 表达从图中读取的约数。

易错点

  • 三条线按学生来源国分类。写 students from the UK studying abroad，方向就清楚。人数图用 the number of 或 the figure，未标数据点用约数。
完整原创范文

The line graph compares the numbers of students from the United States, the United Kingdom and Australia who attended universities in other countries from 2002 to 2007.

Overall, the US had the largest group of overseas students throughout the period, while Australia had the smallest. American and Australian numbers both dipped and then returned to their initial levels, whereas the UK finished substantially below its starting point.

Around 50,000 American students were studying abroad in 2002. The figure fell to approximately 46,000 in 2004 before recovering gradually to 50,000 by 2007. Australia followed a similar pattern on a much smaller scale. Its figure fell from around 10,000 to a low of 8,000 in 2004, then recovered to 10,000 the following year and stayed at that level through 2007.

In contrast, the UK total remained at about 30,000 in 2002 and 2003 and then declined in each subsequent year. It reached approximately 25,000 in 2005 and 21,000 in 2007. By the end, the British figure was a little over twice the Australian total and less than half the American figure.
