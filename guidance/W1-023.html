展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

Overall, television took the largest share for both groups in 2012, but phones were the leading category a decade later. Meanwhile, the proportion allocated to other devices stayed the same for men and women.

  • 总览写男女两组的第一名均从电视变成手机，其他设备占比稳定。主体一按设备对比电视和手机，主体二处理电脑与其他设备。

易错点

  • 每条横条的分母是该组当年的设备使用总时长。可以比较份额，不能从中推出某组实际使用时长更长。
句框 · 按内容填槽

可用句框

The share of [group]’s device time spent on [device] rose from [x]% to [y]%, an increase of [y minus x] percentage points.

完整原创范文

The stacked bars show how men and women distributed their time on electronic devices across television, computers, phones and other devices in 2012 and 2022.

Overall, television took the largest share for both groups in 2012, but phones were the leading category a decade later. Meanwhile, the proportion allocated to other devices stayed the same for men and women.

Television accounted for 45% of men’s device time and half of women’s in 2012. These shares fell to 26% and 27% respectively by 2022. Over the same period, the phone share more than doubled for both groups, rising from 15% to 36% for men and from 18% to 40% for women. Consequently, women allocated a slightly larger share than men to phones in both years.

The changes in computer use were much smaller. The male share edged down from 30% to 28%, while the female figure rose by one percentage point, to 23%. Other devices accounted for exactly one tenth of the total in every bar.
