易错点

  • 适用：表格明确给行动、条件和作用方式，且没有数字。只比较已给信息，不补百分比、调查人数、时间趋势或因果证明；intended说明表列预期机制，不声称已有实验验证。不能改写成“政府应该如何做”的大作文，也不比较表中没有的成本、便利性或绝对效果。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The visual information compares [precise measures] across [groups / dates].

保留准确对象、年份和单位，不要求逐词换同义词。

概述

Overall, [dominant pattern], while [important contrast / exception].

两个槽位要填本图真实特征，流程或地图按阶段、布局变化调整。

同类比较

The figure for [A] was [value and unit], compared with [corresponding value] for [B].

确保年份、指标和分母可比。

跨期变化

[Measure] rose / fell from [start] to [end] between [dates].

静态图不用此句；份额变化与数量变化分清。

完整原创范文

The table outlines four ways consumers can reduce emissions, showing when each action applies and how it is intended to work.

Overall, one measure concerns travel, whereas the other three address electricity use and demand for products or food. The travel option depends on a suitable shared service, while the remaining actions are linked to replacement decisions, repairs or shopping plans.

For transport, using a bus or sharing a ride is presented as an alternative to separate car journeys when an appropriate service is available. The lighting measure applies when worn-out bulbs need replacing. Choosing efficient lighting then reduces the electricity required to provide the same light.

The other measures aim to avoid additional purchases. Repairing a durable item, where this is possible, delays the need to buy a replacement. Planning food purchases before shopping uses a list to avoid buying food that is unlikely to be used. Thus, the conditions differ across the four actions, even though each is described through a change in everyday consumption.
