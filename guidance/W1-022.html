展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

Overall, domestic output and the number of workers both fell, whereas imports increased. By the end of the period, imports exceeded domestic production, reversing the relationship shown in the first year.

  • 总览抓三件事：产量和就业下降，进口增加，最后一个观测年份进口超过产量。正文按单位分两段，保留两图之间的趋势联系。

易错点

  • 吨和人数分别报单位。只知道交叉发生在 2010 年至 2020 年之间，避免猜出精确年份或就业下降原因。
句框 · 按内容填槽

可用句框

By the final year shown, [rising series] had overtaken [falling series], reaching [value] compared with [value] for the latter.

完整原创范文

The upper graph compares steel production and imports in Norland from 1990 to 2020, while the lower graph records employment in the steel industry over the same period.

Overall, domestic output and the number of workers both fell, whereas imports increased. By the end of the period, imports exceeded domestic production, reversing the relationship shown in the first year.

Norland produced 82 million tonnes of steel in 1990, compared with imports of 12 million tonnes. By 2000, output had declined to 70 million tonnes and imports had risen to 25 million. The difference narrowed to 12 million tonnes in 2010, when production stood at 54 million and imports reached 42 million. In 2020, the figures were 40 million and 50 million tonnes respectively.

Employment fell at every point shown, from 190,000 workers in 1990 to 150,000 in 2000. The largest reduction between consecutive observations came in the following decade, when the workforce dropped by 55,000. It then declined further to 64,000 by 2020.
