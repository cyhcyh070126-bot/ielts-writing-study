展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

A、C 在主路北侧，B 在南侧；三处分别靠近居住区、工业区、公园，中心区位于三者之间。

  • 细节一共同描述道路北侧的 A、C，并比较其周边；细节二写 B 与公交站、中心区、工业区的相对位置。
  • 现有地理布局用一般现在时。A、B、C 是可能地点，不能写成已经建成的三个医疗中心。

易错点

  • 题目是客观比较地点，不要求选 A／B／C；不要写 I recommend。
  • 没有比例尺，不能添加 500 metres、ten minutes 等距离或时间。
  • 主路两侧的位置关系不等于交通便利程度；图中未画支路，不声称可直接驾车到达。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The map identifies three possible sites for a new [facility] in [place], together with the surrounding land uses.

possible sites 指备选方案，不是已有设施。

概述

Overall, [sites] lie north of [landmark], while [site] is to its south. The locations differ mainly in their proximity to [surrounding areas].

概括位置差异，不替考官评选最佳方案。

细节一

[Site A] is located immediately south of [feature], to the west of [landmark]. [Site C] occupies a corresponding position on the eastern side, below [feature].

对应位置要以图中相同参照物比较。

细节二

By comparison, [Site B] is situated south of [road] and north of [feature]. [Other landmark] lies to its west, whereas [feature] is across the road.

参照物应明确；图中没有道路接入信息时，不写可驾车直接进入。

完整原创范文

The map identifies three possible sites for a new health centre in Ashford and shows their positions relative to the town centre, main road and surrounding areas of land use.

Overall, sites A and C are both north of the main road, whereas site B lies to the south. The three options are situated near different features: housing in the northwest, a park in the northeast and an industrial area in the southeast.

Site A is immediately south of the residential area and west of the town centre. It is also north of the road that runs from west to east across the town. Site C occupies a corresponding position on the eastern side, lying below the park and to the east of the town centre. Both northern sites are separated from the bus station by the main road.

By comparison, site B is positioned on the southern side of the road, directly north of the industrial area. The bus station lies to its west, while the town centre is across the road to the northwest. Unlike sites A and C, this option is on the same side of the road as the bus station.
