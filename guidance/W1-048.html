展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

两条线共用五个年份，但客流是百万人次、准点率是列车班次百分比。总览写客流先升后降再恢复，2021最低客流与最高准点率同期出现；两个细节段各写一种指标，最后只作同期比较。

易错点

  • 不能把journeys译成独立旅客人数；不能把94%写成94百万人。准点率纵轴80–100%已明确标示，不用两图视觉斜率比较增长速度；也不能自行添加疫情、拥堵减少等原因。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The visual information compares [precise measures] across [groups / dates].

保留准确对象、年份和单位，不要求逐词换同义词。

概述

Overall, [dominant pattern], while [important contrast / exception].

两个槽位要填本图真实特征，流程或地图按阶段、布局变化调整。

同类比较

The figure for [A] was [value and unit], compared with [corresponding value] for [B].

确保年份、指标和分母可比。

跨期变化

[Measure] rose / fell from [start] to [end] between [dates].

静态图不用此句；份额变化与数量变化分清。

完整原创范文

The two line graphs show annual passenger journeys on Sydney trains, measured in millions, and the proportion of services arriving on time in five selected years between 2015 and 2023.

Overall, passenger journeys rose initially, fell sharply in 2021 and then recovered without reaching their earlier peak. Punctuality followed a contrasting pattern around 2021, when the lowest journey total coincided with the highest on-time percentage.

There were 300 million journeys in 2015, increasing to 340 million in 2017 and 380 million in 2019. The total then dropped to 220 million in 2021 before recovering to 360 million in 2023. The final figure was 60 million above the starting level but still 20 million below the peak.

The percentage of trains arriving on time declined from 90% in 2015 to 88% in 2017 and 86% in 2019. It subsequently rose to 94% in 2021, an increase of eight percentage points from the previous observation. By 2023 it had fallen to 89%, one point below the initial rate. The graphs therefore show coinciding movements in these two indicators, but do not establish why those changes occurred.
