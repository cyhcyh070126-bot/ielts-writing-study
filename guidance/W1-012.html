展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

现代语言从第一降到第四，新增的 IT 位居第一。历史和音乐从图中消失，健身与舞蹈占比保持不变。

  • 引言把分母说清。The charts compare the proportions of students enrolled in [type of courses] at [institution] in [periods].
  • 总览先写第一名更替，再概括新增与消失。Overall, [new category] became the largest group, while [formerly offered categories] no longer appeared in the later chart.
  • 主体一把原冠军、消失的课程和新冠军写成一组。先给基期数值，再给今年的变化。
  • 主体二比较两期都有的剩余课程。用 [A] remained unchanged at [x]%, whereas [B] rose by [n] percentage points 写不变与增长。

易错点

  • 图中比例指选课学生的占比。24% 降到 17% 是下降 7 个百分点。图上写 This year，行文用 this year 或 the current year。
完整原创范文

The two pie charts compare the shares of students taking different courses at an adult education centre in 1985 and in the current year.

Overall, modern languages had the largest share in 1985, but IT, which does not appear in the earlier chart, is now the leading subject. Local history and music appreciation are no longer shown, while fitness and dance has retained its share of enrolments.

In 1985, modern languages accounted for nearly a quarter of all students, at 24%. This proportion has since fallen by seven percentage points to 17%. The local history and music appreciation courses represented 15% and 8% of enrolments, respectively, in the earlier chart. Neither appears in the current breakdown, in which IT accounts for 27%, just over a quarter of the total.

Of the other courses included in both charts, fitness and dance ranked second in both periods, attracting 22% of students each time. Cookery rose from 14% to 19%, overtaking both modern languages and art. Art, by contrast, slipped by two percentage points, from 17% in 1985 to 15% this year, giving it the smallest share among the five current categories.
