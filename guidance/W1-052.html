展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

总览先抓男性制造业大降、女性旅游业持续最高；再写性别分工方向。主体一写男性从制造业领先转到零售领先；主体二写女性旅游/健康/零售上升与制造业下降，最后比较制造业性别差距。

  • 总览先抓男性制造业大降、女性旅游业持续最高；再写性别分工方向。主体一写男性从制造业领先转到零售领先；主体二写女性旅游/健康/零售上升与制造业下降，最后比较制造业性别差距。

易错点

  • 适用：同行业、同一性别分母的跨年百分比表。13%男性就业者不等于全镇人口13%；男女百分比不能推出哪一性别人数更多。四类合计不是100%，因此只说所列行业中的最大，不说全镇就业主要由这四类构成；不把每一行加成行业内男女构成。说差距缩小前必须同时核两性的变化，不能由单边下降自动推出。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The visual information compares [precise measures] across [groups / dates].

保留准确对象、年份和单位，不要求逐词换同义词。

概述

Overall, [dominant pattern], while [important contrast / exception].

两个槽位要填本图真实特征，流程或地图按阶段、布局变化调整。

同类比较

The figure for [A] was [value and unit], compared with [corresponding value] for [B].

确保年份、指标和分母可比。

跨期变化

[Measure] rose / fell from [start] to [end] between [dates].

静态图不用此句；份额变化与数量变化分清。

完整原创范文

The table shows the percentages of working men and women employed in four sectors in a town in 1990, 2000 and 2010.

Overall, manufacturing's share of male employment declined substantially, while tourism remained the largest listed sector for women and increased throughout. Women were more concentrated in health and tourism, whereas men consistently had higher proportions in retail and manufacturing.

In 1990, manufacturing employed 30% of working men, more than twice the 13% recorded for either retail or tourism. The manufacturing figure halved to 15% in 2000 and fell further to 10% by 2010. Male retail employment stayed at 13%, while tourism dropped to 10%. Health rose more modestly, from 7% to 9%.

Among women, tourism increased from 23% to 25% and then 27%. Health also grew steadily, from 9% to 15%, and retail edged up from 7% to 9%. Manufacturing initially rose to 8%, before falling to just 3% in 2010. Consequently, the manufacturing gap between men and women narrowed from 23 percentage points to seven.
