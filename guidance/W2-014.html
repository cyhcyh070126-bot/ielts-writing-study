展开审题、观点与写法

不同角度怎样展开

这些是备选论证方向，不是唯一正确立场。选择后要回应题目范围。

  • 原因：当地居民觉得展览缺少变化，或票价、时间和内容不便于日常再访。
  • 措施：策划本地相关的轮换展、社区合作和便于本地人多次使用的票制。
  • 配对：内容缺乏新意对应轮换展，可达性问题对应开放时段与交通；不要只列宣传口号。

本题审题与一种计划

    游客有有限的旅行时间和明确的观光计划，居民可能推迟参观，也可能觉得常设展缺少新内容。给居民更多回访理由，再用合适的开放时间降低到访门槛。

    • 原因一 → 游客停留时间短，会在行程中安排代表性景点 → 居民随时可以去，更容易把参观推迟到以后。对应办法 → 定期在居民空闲时办本地活动，让下一次到访有明确日期。
    • 原因二 → 居民看过常设展，再访的预期收益低 → 家庭更可能安排其他休闲活动。对应办法 → 轮换展览或导览主题，邀请当地学校和社区提供展品，让每次到访都有新内容。

    易错点

    • 第二问的目标是本地居民。提出宣传时，说清走学校、图书馆或社区渠道，并说明活动如何促成到访。只写发展旅游业会偏离对象。
    完整原创范文

    Museums and historic sites are often popular with travellers, while people who live nearby visit less frequently. This difference reflects the limited time tourists have in a destination and the familiarity residents may already have with its attractions. Regular changes to what these sites offer could encourage more local visits.

    For tourists, visiting a city's main attractions is often the reason for making the trip. Someone spending two days in a coastal town may put its historic fort on the itinerary because there will be little opportunity to return. Residents, by contrast, can visit the fort at any time and may keep postponing the trip. Those who have already seen it may also assume that a second visit will repeat the first. A permanent museum exhibition can pose the same problem, particularly when it is unchanged from the one residents remember visiting with their school.

    Museums could invite community groups to help create small exhibitions that change every few months. For example, a museum might display photographs and recordings contributed by residents whose families worked at a local shipyard. A historic fort could offer guided walks on different aspects of its history, with sessions scheduled on weekends when more residents are free. Publicising these programmes through local schools and libraries would make it easier for nearby families to find out what is on. Together, these measures give residents something new to see and a specific date on which to visit.

    In conclusion, tourists have a reason to see these places during a short stay, whereas residents often need a reason to return. Fresh programmes at convenient times can provide that incentive.
