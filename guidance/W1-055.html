易错点

  • 适用：相同分母定义的两年课程份额饼图。35%是5%的七倍，但相对增幅是600%，不是700%；最稳妥可写an increase of 30 percentage points。份额不能推出实际课程总数、学生人数或选课人数增加。不把课程变化归因于就业、设备或学生偏好，因为图里没有这些原因。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The visual information compares [precise measures] across [groups / dates].

保留准确对象、年份和单位，不要求逐词换同义词。

概述

Overall, [dominant pattern], while [important contrast / exception].

两个槽位要填本图真实特征，流程或地图按阶段、布局变化调整。

同类比较

The figure for [A] was [value and unit], compared with [corresponding value] for [B].

确保年份、指标和分母可比。

跨期变化

[Measure] rose / fell from [start] to [end] between [dates].

静态图不用此句；份额变化与数量变化分清。

完整原创范文

The pie charts compare the distribution of courses offered by an adult education centre across five subject areas in 1985 and 2015.

Overall, computing became the largest category, replacing languages, and business also expanded its share. By contrast, languages, arts and practical skills all accounted for smaller proportions in the later year.

In 1985, languages represented 30% of the courses, the highest figure. Arts and practical skills each made up a quarter, together accounting for half of the centre's provision. Business represented a further 15%, while computing was the smallest area at only 5%.

By 2015, computing had risen to 35%, an increase of 30 percentage points and seven times its earlier share. Business rose more moderately to 20%, so these two subjects together comprised 55% of all courses, compared with 20% initially. Languages also stood at 20%, having fallen by ten points. Arts declined to 15%, and practical skills fell to 10%, the lowest proportion in 2015. These figures describe the subject mix rather than the number of students.
