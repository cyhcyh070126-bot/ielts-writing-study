展开审题、观点与写法

不同角度怎样展开

这些是备选论证方向，不是唯一正确立场。选择后要回应题目范围。

  • 在家观看：费用、时间和交通要求较低，远距离观众也能接触节目。
  • 现场观看：共享气氛、即时互动及场景感受难以完整复制。
  • 条件：观看目的与参与障碍不同；可以部分替代，不必断言所有人都会放弃现场。

本题审题与一种计划

    大体不同意。电视观看在成本和可及性上有优势，现场仍有观众与表演者互动、自由观察全场等价值。

    • 支持在家观看 → 门票、长距离出行、照护安排提高到场成本 → 家中观看更容易安排，特写和字幕也能帮人看清表情和听清对白。
    • 反对因此放弃现场 → 现场观众能自行选择观察对象并感受同场反应 → 表演者可按现场笑声或掌声调整节奏 → 演出体验随这场互动变化。
    • 给选择条件 → 以可及性和看清细节为重时，在家可优先。以空间感、集体反应和演出临场变化为重时，去现场仍值得。

    易错点

    • 抓住 better 和 no longer worth attending 的比较。至少写一个同类演出或同一场演出的两种体验，并说明各自适用条件。泛写科技进步或现场票价高，难以回答现场是否已失去价值。
    完整原创范文

    Watching a play or concert at home can be an excellent way to see a performance, especially for people who live far from a suitable venue. However, I largely disagree that television makes attending in person unnecessary. A broadcast improves access, while a live event offers choices and interactions that matter to many audience members.

    The case for watching at home is strongest when attending would be expensive or difficult. A family in a small town might have to buy several tickets, travel to a city and arrange care for a child to see a touring production. A broadcast brings the performance within reach. It can also make some aspects easier to follow. For instance, subtitles can help viewers understand rapid dialogue, and close-up shots can reveal an actor's expression more clearly than a seat at the back of a theatre would.

    In person, however, spectators can decide where to direct their attention. During a stage comedy, someone may watch a silent character's reaction while another actor is speaking, even though a camera would probably remain on the speaker. The audience can also influence the performance itself. An actor may pause after an unexpected burst of laughter, and a musician may respond to an enthusiastic crowd by playing an extra song. These moments arise from the particular combination of performers and audience on the night, which helps explain why people still make the effort to attend.

    In conclusion, television makes performances accessible to more people and sometimes makes them easier to follow. Attending remains worthwhile for viewers who value the freedom to observe the whole stage and share the occasion with the performers and the audience.
