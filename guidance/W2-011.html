展开审题、观点与写法

不同角度怎样展开

这些是备选论证方向，不是唯一正确立场。选择后要回应题目范围。

  • 国际组织：跨境污染需要共享监测和协调标准，单个国家可能缺少合作动力。
  • 各国政府：具体工厂许可、检查、处罚由本国制度执行，能按本地条件调整。
  • 立场：可以主张国际协调与国家执行分工；题干若说instead of，必须评价替代关系。

本题审题与一种计划

    大体不同意。跨境污染需要国际组织协调，各国政府仍应承担本国的主要执行责任。

    • 支持国际组织主责 → 污染可跨境，邻国单独治理会遇到搭便车 → 统一监测与披露标准，可发现污染源并分摊治理成本 → 例如共享河流的数据须能比较。
    • 支持各国政府主责 → 污染源受本国许可与执法体系管辖 → 本国政府能检查、处罚并按各地产业调整措施 → 例如由本国监管者执行工厂排放许可证。

    论证链

    • 支持国际组织主责 → 污染可跨境，邻国单独治理会遇到搭便车 → 统一监测与披露标准，可发现污染源并分摊治理成本 → 例如共享河流的数据须能比较。
    • 支持各国政府主责 → 污染源受本国许可与执法体系管辖 → 本国政府能检查、处罚并按各地产业调整措施 → 例如由本国监管者执行工厂排放许可证。

    易错点

    • 若题目用 single 或 instead of，开头和结尾都要表明对转移主责这一主张的态度。写双方均重要时，也要说清谁协调、谁负责落地。
    完整原创范文

    Environmental damage can cross national borders, which explains the appeal of putting a single international body in charge. I largely disagree with this proposal. An international organisation can coordinate action where countries share a problem, but national governments should retain primary responsibility for enforcing environmental rules at home.

    The strongest argument for international leadership is that a country may have little incentive to tackle pollution whose effects are felt mainly abroad. Consider two countries that share a river. If the upstream country allows factories to discharge waste, much of the cost may fall on communities downstream. A joint agreement overseen by an international body could require both governments to use comparable water tests and publish the results. This would help them identify sources of pollution and agree on how to share the costs of cleaning up the river.

    However, an agreement is effective only when someone can enforce it at individual sites. National regulators can issue permits, inspect factories and impose penalties when businesses exceed legal limits. They can also adapt their approach to local conditions. For example, one country might need to upgrade wastewater treatment in industrial districts, while its neighbour might need to control agricultural runoff. The same regional water standard can apply to both countries even when they use different measures to meet it. Giving domestic authorities clear duties also makes it easier for residents to challenge delays and demand action.

    In conclusion, international institutions should help countries agree on targets and monitor shared environmental risks. Each government should remain accountable for the laws, investment and enforcement needed to achieve those targets within its own borders.
