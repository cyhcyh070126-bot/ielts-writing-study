展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

邮件与当面咨询上升，电话咨询下降；邮件由第二位升至第一，当面咨询始终最低。

  • 细节一比较起始年，并推进到 2022 年邮件与电话持平；细节二描述后两年及末年的差距。
  • 2020—2024 是已结束的时间段，数据描述用一般过去时。引言说明图表内容可用一般现在时。

易错点

  • 交叉点在 2022 年，两条线都是 75；不能提前写成 2021 年邮件最多。
  • 这是每月平均次数，不是全年总次数。不要把 enquiries 写成来访人数。
  • 不要自行解释电话减少是因为人们更喜欢上网，图中没有提供原因。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The graph compares the average monthly number of [items] received through [channels] in [place] from [year] to [year].

补齐对象、地点、单位和时间；number 对应可数的咨询次数。

概述

Overall, [A] and [B] increased, whereas [C] declined. [A] became the leading [category], while [B] remained the least common.

方向与排名各抓一个整体特征；只在图中确实发生时使用 became。

细节一

At the beginning of the period, [C] recorded [figure], compared with [A figure] for [A] and [B figure] for [B]. By [year], [A] and [C] were equal at [figure].

先比较共同起点，再写关键交叉或持平点。

细节二

Thereafter, [A] rose to [figure], while [C] fell to [figure]. [B] increased more gradually, finishing at [figure].

终点数据支持概述；补充差距时先算清单位。

完整原创范文

The line graph compares the average monthly number of enquiries received by a city library by email, by telephone and in person over the five years from 2020 to 2024.

Overall, email and personal enquiries increased, whereas telephone enquiries declined. Email became the most frequently used channel by the end of the period, while visits to the library remained the least common way of making an enquiry.

In 2020, telephone enquiries were the largest category, at 90 per month. This was more than twice the figure for email, which stood at 40, and considerably above the 25 enquiries made in person. Email enquiries then rose to 55 in 2021 and reached 75 in 2022, when they equalled the telephone figure.

During the final two years, email enquiries continued climbing, reaching 100 in 2023 and 130 in 2024. By contrast, the telephone total fell to 65 and then 55. Personal enquiries grew more slowly, from 35 in 2022 to 45 in 2024. Consequently, email exceeded telephone enquiries by 75 per month in the final year.
