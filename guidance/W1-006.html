展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

游客数先降后升，末年最高；平均停留时长先升后降，末年最低。两项整体走向不同，但不能据图断言存在因果。

  • 一个细节段写游客数量，另一个写停留时长；概述同时覆盖两张图，并比较峰值发生年份。
  • 所列年份均已结束，用一般过去时。游客数单位为百万，停留时间单位为晚。

易错点

  • million tourists 与 nights 不能放在同一数值尺度上直接比较。
  • 2021 年游客最少而平均停留最长；两张图不能只写其中一张。
  • 图中只有四个采样年，不能描述未给出的 2020、2022 或 2024 年具体数值。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The bar chart presents [indicator 1] in [place] in [years], while the line graph gives [indicator 2] for the same years.

两个指标都在引言出现，避免遗漏一个面板。

概述

Overall, [indicator 1] ended above its initial level despite an early decline. By contrast, [indicator 2] finished at its lowest point after initially increasing.

每个指标各写一条主特征，再用比较串联。

细节一

[Indicator 1] stood at [x] in [year], before falling to [y]. It then recovered to [z] and reached [final value] in [year].

先后变化只描述已给出的采样年份，不补不存在的中间年。

细节二

Meanwhile, [indicator 2] rose from [x] to [y], then declined to [z] and [final value]. Thus, the year with the most [items] also had the shortest average [duration].

并列关系不等于因果；不要自行写 because。

完整原创范文

The bar chart presents tourist arrivals in Seabourne in 2019, 2021, 2023 and 2025, while the line graph shows the average number of nights that visitors stayed in each of these years.

Overall, arrivals ended considerably above their initial level despite an early fall. Average stays followed a different pattern, rising initially before becoming shorter. The final year had the highest number of tourists but the lowest average length of stay.

In 2019, Seabourne received 1.2 million tourists. This number fell to 0.8 million in 2021, the lowest figure shown, before doubling to 1.6 million in 2023. A further increase brought arrivals to 2 million in 2025. The final total was therefore 0.8 million higher than in 2019 and two and a half times the 2021 figure.

By contrast, the average stay rose from five nights in 2019 to a peak of six in 2021. It subsequently dropped to four nights in 2023 and three in 2025. Consequently, visitors stayed for two fewer nights on average at the end of the period than at the beginning.
