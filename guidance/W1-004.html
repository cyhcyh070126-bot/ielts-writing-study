展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

电器占比上升并取代供暖成为第一；其余三项占比下降。图中没有总用电量，不能判断绝对耗电量增减。

  • 细节一对比供暖和电器两项主要用途；细节二比较烹饪和照明两项较小用途及合计。
  • 两个年份均为过去，用一般过去时；consumption 为不可数名词。

易错点

  • 每个饼合计都为 100%；双饼图要持续比较同一类别的变化。
  • 占比下降不必然表示耗电量下降；缺少总量时不要写 used less electricity。
  • 供暖 45% 降到 30% 是减少 15 个百分点；不是减少 15%。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The charts compare the proportions of household electricity used for [uses] in [place] in [year] and [year].

题目若给的是占比，引言也应使用 proportions 或 shares。

概述

Overall, [A] replaced [B] as the largest category. Its share increased, while the proportions allocated to all other uses declined.

仅适用于其余各项确实全都下降的图。

细节一

In [year], [B] accounted for [x]%, compared with [y]% for [A]. By [year], their shares had changed to [figures], respectively.

两年交叉比较同两项，比整张饼逐块抄写更清楚。

细节二

The shares for [C] and [D] fell from [initial figures] to [final figures]. Together, these categories represented [combined figure]% in the final year.

合计要以相同年份、相同总量为分母。

完整原创范文

The two pie charts compare the distribution of household electricity consumption across heating, cooking, lighting and appliances in a town in 2015 and 2025.

Overall, appliances replaced heating as the largest component of household electricity use. The proportion consumed by appliances increased substantially, whereas the shares of all three other uses became smaller over the ten years.

In 2015, heating represented 45% of the total, making it more than twice as large as the appliance category, which accounted for 20%. By 2025, the position had reversed: appliances took a 45% share, while heating fell to 30%. The increase for appliances was therefore 25 percentage points, compared with a decline of 15 points for heating.

Cooking and lighting each accounted for no more than one fifth of the total in either year. Their respective shares decreased from 20% and 15% in 2015 to 15% and 10% in 2025. Together, these two uses accounted for one quarter of electricity consumption in the final year, down from 35% initially. In contrast, the combined proportion for heating and appliances rose from 65% to 75%, despite the decrease in the heating share.
