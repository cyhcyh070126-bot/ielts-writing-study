完整原创范文

The bar chart compares the average prices paid per kilogram for clothing imported into the European Union from six countries in 1997 and 2003.

Overall, Argentina was the most expensive source in both years, whereas Bangladesh was consistently the cheapest. Prices rose for three suppliers and fell for two, with China recording the largest increase and overtaking Japan.

Among the relatively expensive suppliers, the price of imports from Argentina fell by about £2 per kilogram, from £28 to £26. Brazil remained in second place at approximately £23 in both years, narrowing its gap with Argentina from around £5 to £3. The Japanese figure also fell by £2, from £15 to £13.

China began at about £10 per kilogram, £5 below Japan, but rose to £17 in 2003, by which time it was £4 dearer. Clothing from India also became more expensive, with the average increasing from £7 to £12. The figure for Bangladesh doubled from £3 to £6, yet it remained only half the Indian price in 2003.
