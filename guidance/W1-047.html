易错点

  • 不能把四个比例相加算大学总分；讲师、课程、学生和毕业生不是同一分母。B的博士讲师比例最高也不证明其课程评分或就业最好；图里C恰好在后两项领先。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The visual information compares [precise measures] across [groups / dates].

保留准确对象、年份和单位，不要求逐词换同义词。

概述

Overall, [dominant pattern], while [important contrast / exception].

两个槽位要填本图真实特征，流程或地图按阶段、布局变化调整。

同类比较

The figure for [A] was [value and unit], compared with [corresponding value] for [B].

确保年份、指标和分母可比。

跨期变化

[Measure] rose / fell from [start] to [end] between [dates].

静态图不用此句；份额变化与数量变化分清。

完整原创范文

The table compares four percentage indicators at three fictional Canadian universities in 2025, covering staff qualifications, course ratings, completion and graduate employment.

Overall, no university led on all four measures. B had the highest proportion of lecturers with doctorates and the best completion rate, whereas C led on course ratings and employment. A occupied the middle position on every indicator.

At B, 90% of lecturers held a PhD, compared with 75% at A and 60% at C. The same ranking applied to course completion: the figures were 92%, 88% and 84% respectively. The gap between B and C was therefore much wider for staff qualifications, at 30 percentage points, than for completion, at eight points.

The pattern was reversed for the other two measures. At C, 90% of courses received high ratings, against 80% at A and 70% at B. Employment six months after graduation was also highest at C, at 88%, followed by A with 82% and B with 76%. Thus, the institution with the lowest figures for staff qualifications and completion recorded the strongest results on the remaining indicators.
