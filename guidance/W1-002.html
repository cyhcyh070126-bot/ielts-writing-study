展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

年龄较大的组去电影院、体育中心的比例更低，博物馆则相反；年轻组电影院最高，最年长组博物馆最高。

  • 细节一把电影院和体育中心合写，比较其相似的年龄差异；细节二写方向相反的博物馆并比较各组内部排名。
  • 统计发生在 2025 年一个月内，用一般过去时。不同年龄组不是同一群人随着年龄增长的时间轨迹。

易错点

  • 70% 与 65% 相差 5 个百分点；写 percentage points 比直接写 5% 更准确。
  • 每人可以去多个场所，因此同一年龄组三个百分比不必相加为 100%。
  • 不要使用 increased over time：图表只有一个统计月份，没有时间序列。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The chart compares the proportions of [population] in [age groups] who visited [venues] in [place] during [period].

明确百分比的分母是各年龄组居民。

概述

Overall, [A] and [B] were more popular among younger residents, while [C] was visited by a larger share of the oldest group.

描述组间差异，不把静态年龄比较伪装成时间趋势。

细节一

For [A], the figure was [x]% among [group 1], compared with [y]% and [z]% in the other two groups. [B] displayed a similar pattern, at [figures], respectively.

respectively 的三项顺序必须与前文年龄组对应。

细节二

The pattern for [C] was different: [figures]. It was therefore the leading venue among [group], but the least frequently visited among [group].

用同组内部比较支撑概述，而非添加个人评价。

完整原创范文

The bar chart compares the percentages of Riverton residents in three age groups who visited a cinema, a museum or a sports centre during one month in 2025.

Overall, the cinema and sports centre attracted larger proportions of younger residents, whereas museums were more popular with older people. The cinema was the leading venue among the youngest group, while museums ranked first among those aged 50 and over.

Among people aged 18 to 29, 70% visited a cinema and 65% used a sports centre. The corresponding figures for the middle age group were lower, at 55% and 50%. These two venues were visited by only 30% and 35% of the oldest residents, respectively. The difference between cinema and sports centre attendance was five percentage points in every age group.

Museum attendance showed the opposite pattern. Only 25% of the youngest residents visited a museum, compared with 40% of those aged 30 to 49 and 60% of the oldest group. Thus, the museum figure for people aged 50 and above was twice their cinema figure and 25 percentage points higher than their sports centre figure.
