展开审题、观点与写法

不同角度怎样展开

这些是备选论证方向，不是唯一正确立场。选择后要回应题目范围。

  • 小班：教师能听到更多学生的语言输出，逐人反馈和练习机会较充足。
  • 人数影响有限：明确任务和分组活动可改善大班互动，某些共用输入任务并不依赖小班。
  • 判断：比较的是语言学习成效，需说明课堂目标、教师反馈与人数如何相互影响。

本题审题与一种计划

    班级人数对个别反馈和口语训练影响较大。教师可以用合理的活动设计减轻大班的限制，尤其在阅读等任务中。

    • 支持小班 → 教师能听到每位学生的发音 → 及时发现反复出现的错误并让学生再试一次 → 适合需要逐人反馈的口语课。
    • 支持人数影响有限 → 同一时间分组完成多轮对话 → 每名学生可以获得相近的练习时间 → 如果重点是阅读或词汇，教师也能通过共用任务检查理解。
    • 判断标准 → 先看课堂目标与反馈方式。用大班讲解共同内容，再为需要个别反馈的任务安排小组活动。

    易错点

    • 题目聚焦语言课。讨论大班可行时，写清分组任务如何提供练习机会，并交代个别纠错的时间限制。写降低成本可以解释可行性，仍需交代对学习效果的影响。
    完整原创范文

    The importance of class size in language teaching depends partly on what learners are expected to do. Some people favour small groups, while others believe that effective teaching can produce similar results in a larger class. In my view, smaller classes are particularly valuable when learners need individual feedback on their speaking.

    The case for small classes is strongest when the teacher needs to listen to each student. In a pronunciation lesson, for example, a teacher with eight learners can ask everyone to use a target sound in a sentence, identify recurring mistakes and invite each learner to try again. Students who rarely volunteer can also be given a turn. With thirty learners, it is harder to do all of this in the same period, so some students may leave the lesson unaware of a mistake that affects how clearly they speak.

    However, classroom organisation can make the number of students less important. If a teacher divides a large class into pairs and assigns each pair a task, such as exchanging information to complete a timetable, many learners can speak at the same time. A large class can also work well for reading lessons in which everyone completes the same comprehension exercise and the teacher reviews common misunderstandings. The key limitation of these methods is that the teacher cannot easily give detailed feedback to every individual.

    Overall, I would use larger classes to teach shared material and smaller groups for activities that require individual correction. This approach makes it possible to teach more students while preserving personal feedback at the points where it matters most.
