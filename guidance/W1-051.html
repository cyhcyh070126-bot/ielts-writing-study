易错点

  • 适用：同一单位的多序列年际价格图。最高/最低要加generally或usually保留交叉例外，不能说日本每年都最高、美国每年都最低。数据来自图上约读，数值用about/approximately/roughly；别把2.85写成准确到美分的实测值。不猜汇率、进口政策或消费原因。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The visual information compares [precise measures] across [groups / dates].

保留准确对象、年份和单位，不要求逐词换同义词。

概述

Overall, [dominant pattern], while [important contrast / exception].

两个槽位要填本图真实特征，流程或地图按阶段、布局变化调整。

同类比较

The figure for [A] was [value and unit], compared with [corresponding value] for [B].

确保年份、指标和分母可比。

跨期变化

[Measure] rose / fell from [start] to [end] between [dates].

静态图不用此句；份额变化与数量变化分清。

完整原创范文

The line graph compares banana prices in Japan, France, Germany and the USA between 1994 and 2004, measured in US dollars per kilogram.

Overall, Japan generally had the highest prices and the largest fluctuations, although France briefly overtook it in 2001. American prices were usually the lowest and became particularly stable in the later years.

In Japan, the price rose from about $1.9 in 1994 to just under $2.9 in 1995, before returning to around $2 the following year. It then fluctuated, falling to approximately $1.6 in 2001 and recovering to about $2.6 by 2004. France remained near $1.7 during the first four years, but subsequently varied more widely. Its price reached roughly $1.8 in 2001, then fell to around $1 in 2003 before rebounding to approximately $1.6.

German prices moved within a narrower band of about $1.2 to $1.6, ending near the lower limit. In the USA, the figure increased from roughly $0.8 initially to about $1.2 in 1995. It stayed close to $1.1 from 1999 onwards.
