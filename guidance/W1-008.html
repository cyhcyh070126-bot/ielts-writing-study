展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

公园新增或扩大活动、服务设施；池塘和南侧入口保持不变。中部草坪变为滑板场，北部花园变为咖啡馆。

  • 细节一写北部的保留、替换和扩建；细节二写中央与南部，并比较未变的入口。
  • 2005 年原状用过去时；现在布局用现在时，变化可用现在完成时被动 has been replaced／has been added。

易错点

  • has been replaced by 中，前面是旧设施，by 后面是新设施。
  • 不能把池塘写成新建；它在两张图的西北角完全相同。
  • 不要写新设施让居民更健康或吸引更多游客；图上只有空间变化。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The maps compare the layout of [place] in [past year] with its present arrangement.

若题目给未来规划，则应改成 proposed arrangement。

概述

Overall, the park has gained [types of facilities], while [unchanged features] have remained in place. The main changes involve the replacement of [A] and the expansion of [B].

概述抓主要变化和显著保留项，不逐个罗列方位。

细节一

In the northern section, [A] has been replaced by [B], while [C] remains in the [direction]. To the [direction], [D] has been enlarged.

被替换的是旧设施；replace 后的主客体不要颠倒。

细节二

The former [A] in the centre is now occupied by [B]. A new [C] has also been added in the [direction], whereas [D] is unchanged.

former 表示原来的；设施位置按北箭头判断。

完整原创范文

The two maps compare the layout of Greenfield Park in 2005 with its current arrangement in 2026, showing changes to the facilities within the same park boundary.

Overall, the park has acquired additional recreational and service facilities, with the main changes affecting its northern and central areas. However, the pond and the southern entrance have remained in their original positions.

In the northern part of the park, the flower garden that previously stood between the pond and the playground has been replaced by a cafe. The pond itself is unchanged in the northwest. Meanwhile, the playground in the northeastern corner has been enlarged, extending farther west and south than it did in 2005. It now occupies a noticeably larger section of the park.

The central lawn has also disappeared, and a skate park now occupies the same rectangular space. A bicycle parking area has been added in the southwest, to the west of the entrance. The entrance continues to be situated in the middle of the southern boundary. Unlike the other newly developed facilities, it has not been moved or enlarged.
