易错点

  • 适用：同一图有已报数据和预测数据。预测段用is projected/forecast/expected，不把2030–2050写成已发生的过去式。每年三个年龄份额加100%，但人口总数不和百分比相加。15–64岁只是年龄组，不等于所有人都在就业；儿童份额下降不能单凭这一点推出儿童人数下降。增长放缓也不等于人口减少。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The visual information compares [precise measures] across [groups / dates].

保留准确对象、年份和单位，不要求逐词换同义词。

概述

Overall, [dominant pattern], while [important contrast / exception].

两个槽位要填本图真实特征，流程或地图按阶段、布局变化调整。

同类比较

The figure for [A] was [value and unit], compared with [corresponding value] for [B].

确保年份、指标和分母可比。

跨期变化

[Measure] rose / fell from [start] to [end] between [dates].

静态图不用此句；份额变化与数量变化分清。

完整原创范文

The two line graphs show population size and the age composition of the fictional country Latoa from 2010 to 2050. Figures after 2020 are projections.

Overall, the population is expected to keep growing, although the increases become smaller. People aged 15–64 remain the largest group throughout, while the share of children falls and that of older people rises.

The total population increased from 2 million in 2010 to 2.4 million in 2020. It is projected to reach 2.8 million in 2030 and 3.3 million by 2050, with growth slowing across the forecast period. The final figure would be 1.3 million above the starting level.

In terms of age, the proportion aged 15–64 declined slightly, from 60% to 59%, and is forecast to fall to 54% in 2050. Children under 15 accounted for 30% initially, compared with 10% for those aged 65 and over. These shares are expected to reach 20% and 26% respectively. The two groups are projected to be equal, at 22%, in 2040.
