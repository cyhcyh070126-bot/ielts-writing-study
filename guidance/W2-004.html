展开审题、观点与写法

不同角度怎样展开

这些是备选论证方向，不是唯一正确立场。选择后要回应题目范围。

  • 优势链：减少通勤→省出连续工作或照顾家庭的时间→提升可安排性。
  • 劣势链：沟通依赖线上信息→新人难得到即时指导→协作错误或孤立感增加。
  • 条件：岗位的独立程度、居家空间和团队反馈安排；只问利弊时不必强行下哪边更大的结论。

本题审题与一种计划

  • 范围是员工，不是只讨论雇主节省办公室租金。
  • 分别解释居家办公的优势与劣势。
  • 题目未要求判断哪方更大；结尾概括两面即可。

按题目分析员工获得的灵活性与失去边界、交流的风险，不强加利大于弊的比较任务。

  • 引言：改写安排，点出主要收益与代价。
  • 主体一：说明节省通勤时间、安排生活与专注工作的优势。
  • 主体二：说明工作生活边界模糊与缺少即时同事支持。
  • 结论：概括两面，并指出具体体验受工作条件影响。

论证链

  • 取消通勤 → 时间与精力得到释放 → 员工更易安排生活或专注任务 → 体现个人层面的优势。
  • 工作设备始终在身边且缺少同事即时交流 → 下班仍工作、问题难及时解决 → 员工承受过劳与孤立风险。

易错点

  • 题目只问员工，主体却主要写公司省租金。
  • 把利弊题自动写成outweigh题，花大量篇幅比较却没有充分解释两面。
  • 罗列五六个优缺点，每个都只有几个词。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

[Arrangement] is becoming a common option for [the group named in the question]. It offers [main advantage], but can also lead to [main disadvantage].

不必机械加入I believe the advantages outweigh the disadvantages。

主体一

One important advantage for [specified group] is [benefit]. [Explain how it arises]. For example, [specific situation]. This can [immediate result]. Another related benefit is [a distinct benefit or further development].

每个优点都说明收益如何发生，并始终围绕题目限定的受影响者。

主体二

On the other hand, [arrangement] can create [cost]. [Explain the mechanism]. For instance, [realistic illustration]. A further difficulty is [another cost]. This may [consequence for the specified group].

缺点不是一句相反的话，而是独立的具体问题及其后果。

结论

In conclusion, [arrangement] can provide [benefit], while also creating [cost]. The experience of [group] will depend partly on [relevant practical conditions].

概括已分析的内容即可，避免结尾增加未讨论的新利弊。

完整原创范文

Working mainly from home changes both the practical organisation of an employee's day and the social experience of having a job. The arrangement can give workers greater control over their time, but it can also make boundaries and professional relationships harder to maintain.

A major advantage is the removal of the daily commute. Employees can use the time previously spent travelling to prepare meals, exercise or take children to school, rather than beginning the working day already tired. For example, someone who once needed two connecting trains may be able to start work after a short walk instead. Home working can also make demanding tasks easier when employees have a quiet room and can control interruptions. A writer preparing a detailed report may concentrate more effectively without frequent conversations around an open-plan office, producing better work with less stress.

However, the same arrangement can weaken the separation between employment and private life. When a laptop is always within reach, workers may continue checking messages after dinner or feel obliged to respond simply because they are technically available. This is especially difficult for people who share a small home and cannot close a door on their workspace at the end of the day. Employees may also lose informal support from colleagues, such as the opportunity to ask a quick question or learn by observing an experienced teammate. New staff can consequently feel isolated or spend much longer solving a problem that a short office conversation would have resolved.

In conclusion, home working offers employees useful time savings and potential gains in concentration, while bringing risks of overwork and isolation that vary with their housing, role and workplace support.
