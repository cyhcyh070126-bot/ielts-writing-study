展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

先比较两性的活动排名，再把三项男性较高与一项女性较高分组。总览抓“邮件最多、求职最少、健康信息是例外”；细节用百分点支持最大的新闻差距和最小的求职差距。

易错点

  • 适用：同一年、同一分母定义下的男女百分比比较。不是趋势图，不写rose/fell；不能把60%与49%的差写成11%。分母是大不列颠16岁及以上的各性别成年人，不是全部英国网民。四类可重叠，不能加总成100%或无人数权重地算男女总体均值。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The visual information compares [precise measures] across [groups / dates].

保留准确对象、年份和单位，不要求逐词换同义词。

概述

Overall, [dominant pattern], while [important contrast / exception].

两个槽位要填本图真实特征，流程或地图按阶段、布局变化调整。

同类比较

The figure for [A] was [value and unit], compared with [corresponding value] for [B].

确保年份、指标和分母可比。

跨期变化

[Measure] rose / fell from [start] to [end] between [dates].

静态图不用此句；份额变化与数量变化分清。

完整原创范文

The bar chart compares the percentages of men and women aged 16 and over in Great Britain who used the internet for four selected activities in 2013.

Overall, emailing was the most common activity for both sexes, whereas looking for work was the least common. Men recorded higher proportions in three categories, with health information being the only exception.

Email was used by 78% of men and 72% of women, a difference of six percentage points. Reading or downloading online news ranked second for each group, at 60% and 49% respectively. This eleven-point gap was the largest difference between the sexes among the four activities.

The pattern was reversed for health information: 46% of women sought it online, compared with 39% of men. By contrast, the figures for job seeking were relatively close, at 25% for men and 22% for women. The three-point difference here was the smallest gender gap. In each group, the proportion using email was more than three times the share using the internet to look for a job.
