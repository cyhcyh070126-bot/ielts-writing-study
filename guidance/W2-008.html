展开审题、观点与写法

不同角度怎样展开

这些是备选论证方向，不是唯一正确立场。选择后要回应题目范围。

  • 积极链：容器多次使用→每次购买所需的新包装减少→垃圾处理和材料需求可能下降。
  • 代价链：清洗、运输和卫生管理也有资源成本，不能只看少一个包装。
  • 判断条件：重复使用次数、清洗方式和配送距离；用具体场景解释净收益，不编环保百分比。

本题审题与一种计划

  • 评价这种购物方式的发展，而不是一般性的环保重要性。
  • 解释重复使用容器如何减少包装与改变消费习惯。
  • 给出明确积极判断；可承认操作条件，但不把全文写成没有结论的利弊清单。

总体积极，因为可以减少重复包装并支持按需购买；卫生与标签规则是实现收益的条件。

  • 引言：说明变化，明确总体积极。
  • 主体一：解释容器复用如何减少包装需求与废物。
  • 主体二：解释按需购买和商品信息如何改善习惯，交代卫生操作条件。
  • 结论：综合环境收益与可执行性，重申积极判断。

论证链

  • 同一容器反复使用 → 新包装需求下降 → 家庭分类处理的包装减少 → 环境和日常管理收益。
  • 允许按需要购买少量 → 降低试购和过量购买的浪费 → 配合透明标签与卫生流程 → 积极收益可实际实现。

易错点

  • 只说环保重要，没有解释补充装这种方式如何起作用。
  • 声称完全没有包装或能源消耗，忽略运输与商店操作。
  • 全文写双方都有道理，却不回答positive or negative。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The move towards [specific development] changes the way [activity] is carried out. I regard it as a positive development because [benefit one] and [benefit two].

评价对象要具体，不写与题目无关的时代发展套话。

主体一

The clearest benefit is [specific improvement]. [Explain how the development produces it]. For example, [realistic illustration]. This reduces/improves [immediate effect]. Over repeated use, [further consequence].

说明收益产生的过程，避免仅写它有利于环境。

主体二

A further advantage is [a different improvement]. [Explain its effect on behaviour or decisions]. For instance, [illustration]. To achieve this benefit, [necessary safeguard]. With this condition in place, [explain why the overall judgement remains positive].

合理条件可以增强判断，不必把每个风险写成同等分量的反对段。

结论

In conclusion, I consider [development] beneficial overall, provided that [relevant condition], because it can [synthesise the improvements].

条件若在结尾出现，主体段应已经解释过。

完整原创范文

Refill shopping allows customers to reuse a container instead of taking home fresh packaging whenever a product runs out. I regard this as a positive development because it can reduce avoidable waste and encourage more deliberate decisions about everyday purchases.

The most direct benefit is that a durable container can perform the same job repeatedly. A family buying washing liquid, for example, can refill an existing bottle rather than discard one and replace it with an almost identical bottle every month. This avoids some of the material and energy needed to manufacture and transport separate packages. It also reduces the quantity of packaging that households must sort, store and put out for collection. Although refill systems still require containers for deliveries to the shop, the opportunity to use these repeatedly can make the overall arrangement less wasteful than treating every purchase as a completely new package.

Refill shopping can also encourage customers to buy quantities that suit their actual needs. Someone trying a new food can purchase a small amount rather than commit to a large packet that may eventually be thrown away. Clear prices and product information allow shoppers to compare options while paying attention to what they consume, instead of relying mainly on attractive packaging. Shops do need sensible hygiene procedures, accurate labels and a reliable way of measuring the product. These practical requirements can be managed through staff training and clear instructions, allowing the environmental and behavioural benefits to emerge without making the system unnecessarily complicated for customers.

In conclusion, refill options are a welcome change when they are well managed, since they can reduce repeated packaging and help people purchase only what they expect to use.
