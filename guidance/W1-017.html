展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

两段短程以步行为主，2–5 英里汽车略领先，超过 5 英里以公交为主。步行比例随距离下降，公交呈相反分布，自行车一直占较小比例。

  • 引言明确对象、四种方式与距离分组。The chart compares the proportions of [group] using [modes], grouped by [distance or other variable].
  • 总览把短途、长途和过渡区分开。Overall, [mode] was most common over short distances, whereas [mode] had the largest share in the longest distance band.
  • 主体一写步行与公交的相反分布，挑最短和最长的数字。用 fell as journey distance increased 表示沿横轴的空间比较。
  • 主体二写汽车和自行车。找汽车在哪一档最高，再比较自行车最高和最低的距离段。

易错点

  • 距离段用 over five miles、between two and five miles。写柱图中的距离差异时用 for longer journeys，避免把距离误写成时间变化。
完整原创范文

The chart compares four ways in which UK children aged 11 to 16 travelled to school over the course of one year, with journeys divided into four distance bands.

Overall, walking was the leading option for the two shortest bands, while the bus dominated the longest journeys. The car had a slight lead in the two-to-five-mile group, and cycling accounted for a relatively small share at every distance.

Walking represented 85% of journeys of up to a mile and 61% of those between one and two miles. Its share fell to 25% in the next band, and no journeys of more than five miles were made on foot. The pattern for bus travel was reversed. It accounted for just 1% of the shortest journeys, but its share rose to 33% in the two-to-five-mile group and reached 66% beyond five miles.

Car use rose from 5% for the shortest journeys to a peak of 35% in the two-to-five-mile band, narrowly ahead of the bus at that distance. The figure then eased to 30% for the longest trips. Cycling varied less, ranging from 4% to 8%.
