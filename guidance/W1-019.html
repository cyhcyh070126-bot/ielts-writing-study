展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

Overall, the park gained several new facilities, including a playground and a visitor centre. The main path still crossed the park from west to east, and the pond remained in the south-west.

  • 总览先说设施种类增加，再指出公园的主轴保留。主体按主路南北两侧分段，同时比较同一区域的两个年份。

易错点

  • 野餐区仍在原来的东北区域，只是变小。按图写 was reduced in size。林地完全被社区花园替代，两类变化应分开写。
句框 · 按内容填槽

可用句框

[Feature] remained in the same location, but its area was reduced to make room for [new feature].

完整原创范文

The two maps compare the layout of a municipal park in 1988 and 2001, showing how the land on either side of its main path was used.

Overall, the park gained several new facilities, including a playground and a visitor centre. The main path still crossed the park from west to east, and the pond remained in the south-west.

In 1988, a woodland occupied the north-west corner, with a large picnic field on the other side of the northern half. By 2001, the woodland had been replaced by a community garden of the same size. The picnic field was kept, although it was reduced to about half its former area. A playground was built immediately to its east, on land previously used for picnics.

South of the path, the pond was unchanged. The former car park in the south-east was divided into two areas, with cycle parking on the western side and a visitor centre on the eastern side. No car park was shown in the later layout.
