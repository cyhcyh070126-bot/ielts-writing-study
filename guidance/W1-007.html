展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

共七个主要阶段，从收集废纸到形成新纸卷；前半段准备与制浆，后半段清洁并成纸。

  • 细节一写收集、分拣、切碎与制浆；细节二写清洁、压片、烘干与卷纸。
  • 一般生产过程用一般现在时。纸张是处理对象，多用被动语态；设备执行动作时可以用主动语态。

易错点

  • 图中没有回到起点的箭头，所以不能把这张图称为闭合循环。
  • 图中没有温度、耗时或化学药剂，不要为了凑字数自行补入。
  • paper、water、pulp 通常为不可数；阶段顺序比堆砌复杂词更重要。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The diagram shows how [input material] is recycled to produce [final product].

输入与产物都须与图一致。

概述

Overall, the process consists of [number] main stages, beginning with [first step] and ending with [last step]. The material is first prepared and then converted into [product].

阶段数按主框计；不要把每个箭头或辅助输入都算一步。

细节一

First, [material] is collected and [action]. After [unwanted material] has been removed, it is [next action]. [Input] is then added to form [intermediate product].

顺序词跟随箭头；has been removed 表示进入下一步前已处理完。

细节二

Next, [intermediate product] is cleaned before being [action]. Finally, it passes through [equipment], where [final action] takes place.

where 的先行词应为设备或地点，不要让指代含糊。

完整原创范文

The diagram shows how used paper is recycled through a sequence of processing stages to produce new rolls of paper that can subsequently be used again.

Overall, there are seven main stages, starting with the collection of used paper and finishing with drying and rolling. The first part prepares the waste and turns it into pulp, while the later stages clean this mixture and form new paper.

Initially, used paper is brought from collection points to the recycling facility. It is then sorted so that unsuitable materials, including plastic and metal, can be removed. The selected paper is cut into small pieces in the shredding stage. After this, water is added and the mixture is stirred to create pulp, ready for further treatment.

The pulp is next cleaned to remove ink and any remaining impurities. Once this has been completed, it is pressed into sheets, with excess water squeezed out during the same stage. Finally, the sheets pass over heated rollers, which dry the paper. The dry sheets are then wound into rolls, bringing the recycling process to an end.
