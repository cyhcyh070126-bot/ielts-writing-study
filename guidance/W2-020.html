    句框 · 按内容填槽

    可用句框

    Supporters of [schedule A] point out that it fits [an existing arrangement]. Moving to [schedule B] could therefore require [an adjustment].

    可用句框

    I favour [schedule B] where [the adjustment] is feasible, because it directly improves [the intended outcome] for [the affected group].

    完整原创范文

    There is disagreement over whether secondary schools should move their first lessons to a later time. Early starts can help families and schools coordinate their daily routines. Nevertheless, I favour a modest delay where transport and supervision can be arranged, because it gives pupils a better chance of being ready to learn.

    An early start can fit existing travel and care arrangements. Some parents take their children to school on the way to work, and a later opening would leave them with a gap between the time they need to leave and the time their children can enter the building. An early finish can also leave more daylight for outdoor activities or more time for pupils who help care for a sibling. In communities where several schools share a small bus fleet, changing one timetable can force changes at the others. These are real costs to consider before setting a new start time.

    On the other hand, an early timetable can make the first lesson difficult for pupils who have to travel a long way. A pupil who leaves home shortly after waking may arrive hungry and still be settling in when the teacher introduces a new topic. Starting thirty minutes later could allow time for breakfast and a less hurried journey. A school could keep a supervised room open at its previous opening time for families that need it, while starting lessons later for everyone. This would retain some of the convenience of the earlier timetable and make the opening lesson more useful to pupils who benefit from the extra time.

    In conclusion, an early start simplifies existing arrangements, but a later first lesson can better serve the educational purpose of the school day. I would support the change where a school can provide practical arrangements for those who still need to arrive early.
