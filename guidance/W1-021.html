完整原创范文

The diagram illustrates the treatment of river water for supply through public taps, together with the routes taken by solid material removed during the process.

Overall, the water is screened, settled, filtered and treated with ultraviolet light before being stored and supplied. The two solid waste routes branch off during the initial treatment stages, before the water enters the sand filter.

To begin with, river water is sent through a coarse screen. Material caught by the screen is separated from the water and taken away for disposal. The water that passes through the screen then enters a settling tank. Here, sediment is separated out and sent to a drying bed, while the water continues along the main route.

The next stage takes place in a sand filter, through which the water passes before entering a UV chamber. Once the ultraviolet treatment is complete, the treated water is transferred to a storage tank. From there, it is supplied to the public taps.
