易错点

  • 改造前后沿同一条走廊比较。原位替换用 was converted into。只有功能或设施确实换位置时才用 was moved。

完整原创范文

The two floor plans compare the internal layout of a town library in 2004 with its layout twenty years later.

Overall, the building was reorganised to provide more space for digital and group study and to make borrowing self-service. However, the main corridor, the entrance hall and the reading room remained in the same positions.

North of the corridor, the reading room continued to occupy the western end of the library. The archive in the middle of this side was converted into a computer lab, while the staff office at the eastern end made way for group study rooms. These changes meant that two of the three rooms on this side were given new functions.

On the south side, the entrance hall remained in the centre, with the facilities on either side being altered. In the south-west, self-service kiosks took the place of the borrowing desk. The bookshop in the south-east, opposite the former staff office, was replaced by a cafe.
