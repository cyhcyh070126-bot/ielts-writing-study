展开审题、观点与写法

不同角度怎样展开

这些是备选论证方向，不是唯一正确立场。选择后要回应题目范围。

  • 同罚角度：严重伤害对受害者的影响不因犯罪者年龄而消失，处罚也具有保护社会的作用。
  • 区别角度：未成年人的判断、受控制程度和改过条件可能不同，机械同罚不能反映这些因素。
  • 条件：责任、案情、风险和康复措施共同决定回应；不把讨论青少年等同于纵容重罪。

本题审题与一种计划

    大体不同意。严重伤害可以对应严厉处罚，法院仍应根据年龄、责任程度和风险选择合适的量刑与矫治方案。

    • 支持同罚 → 受害人所受的伤害与犯人年龄无关 → 相同的量刑尺度表达对严重伤害的一致态度 → 有预谋的暴力袭击等情形中，严厉刑罚也能隔离高风险犯人。
    • 反对一律同罚 → 年龄、受胁迫程度和犯罪角色影响可归责性 → 法官应分别评估伤害、责任和未来风险 → 以适当时长的安全羁押加教育辅导降低释放后的再犯风险。

    论证链

    • 支持同罚 → 受害人所受的伤害与犯人年龄无关 → 相同的量刑尺度表达对严重伤害的一致态度 → 有预谋的暴力袭击等情形中，严厉刑罚也能隔离高风险犯人。
    • 反对一律同罚 → 年龄、受胁迫程度和犯罪角色影响可归责性 → 法官应分别评估伤害、责任和未来风险 → 以适当时长的安全羁押加教育辅导降低释放后的再犯风险。

    易错点

    • 抓住 serious 和 same 两个词。论证应落到重罪的量刑尺度和实际处罚方式，举例可用抢劫或严重袭击。只写青少年犯罪为何增加会偏离这一问法。
    完整原创范文

    When a young person commits a serious offence, some people argue that the sentence should be the same as it would be for an adult. I largely disagree. The harm caused should carry considerable weight, but courts should also examine how the offender became involved and which sentence would protect the public over time.

    The argument for equal penalties is strongest in cases involving deliberate violence. An attack can leave a victim unable to work or afraid to leave home, regardless of the offender's age. A sentence that appears lenient can make victims feel that the harm they suffered has been overlooked. Where a teenager planned an armed robbery and remains a danger to others, a substantial period in secure custody may therefore be justified. Courts should explain how they have taken the seriousness of the crime into account.

    Even in serious cases, however, young offenders may differ considerably in their responsibility and prospects for rehabilitation. A sixteen-year-old recruited by an older gang member may have acted under pressure, while the person who planned the robbery may have had much more control. An age-appropriate sentence can recognise these differences while protecting the community. For example, a court could order secure detention that includes schooling and counselling, followed by supervised release with requirements to attend training and avoid former gang contacts. Those conditions address risks that would still exist after a prison sentence ended.

    In conclusion, serious crimes by young people can warrant substantial penalties. Courts should assess the offender's role and future risk alongside the harm done, and use those findings to decide the type and length of punishment.
