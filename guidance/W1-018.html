展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

欧洲两类教育参与率均最高，撒哈拉以南非洲均最低。各地区大学年龄组的高等教育参与率都低于对应中学年龄组的中学参与率，性别差距方向因地区和阶段而异。

  • 引言分清两个年龄组。The first chart compares [school-aged group], while the second focuses on [college-aged group].
  • 总览写两张图共有的地区极值和整体高低差，再决定是否有必要提性别方向变化。
  • 主体一比较中学参与率。先写最高与最低，再比较中间两地区的男女差距方向。
  • 主体二比较高等教育参与率。先写欧洲的领先幅度，再比较其他三个地区，最后指出一个清晰的性别差异。

易错点

  • 中学图分母是中学年龄组，高教图分母是大学年龄组。可比较 participation rates，无法从两张图计算同一批学生的升学率。
完整原创范文

The charts show participation in secondary education among school-aged boys and girls and in higher education among college-aged men and women in four regions in 2000.

Overall, Europe recorded the highest rates at both levels, while Sub-Saharan Africa had the lowest. The percentages in higher education were lower than the corresponding secondary-school figures in every region, and the gender gap did not always favour the same group.

At secondary level, European participation reached about 96% among boys and 100% among girls. In Sub-Saharan Africa, roughly a third of boys and a fifth of girls attended. The two remaining regions fell between these extremes. Girls in Latin America were ahead of boys by about ten percentage points, at 60% versus 50%, while in East Asia boys led by roughly five points.

In higher education, participation in Europe was about 70% among women and 60% among men. The next-highest rates were in Latin America, where around one fifth of men and 15% of women participated. Neither group exceeded 10% in East Asia, and the corresponding figures for Sub-Saharan Africa were lower still, at approximately 6% for men and 4% for women.
