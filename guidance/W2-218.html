句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

Difficulty in [specific situation] can create serious problems for [group]. The main consequences concern [problem areas], and [responsible actors] can respond through [broad measures].

回答问题与措施，不必硬加同意程度。

主体一

One consequence is [problem]. When [situation], [explain how the harm occurs]. For example, [concrete illustration]. A further problem is [second consequence]. This can [further impact on the group or society].

把原因与结果区分清楚：这段写已经发生的困难会造成什么。

主体二

To address [problem one], [actor] should [specific action]. This would [explain how the action reduces the harm]. For example, [implementation detail]. [Actor] could also [measure targeting problem two]. [Explain the intended effect and a necessary condition].

措施要有执行者、动作和作用机制，与前文问题逐项对应。

结论

In conclusion, [situation] can lead to [main harms]. [Measure one] and [measure two] would help by [brief explanation of the shared purpose].

避免在结尾突然补充第三个重大问题或措施。
