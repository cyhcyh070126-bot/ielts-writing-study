展开审题、观点与写法

不同角度怎样展开

这些是备选论证方向，不是唯一正确立场。选择后要回应题目范围。

  • 停止播报角度：反复突出暴力细节可能加重恐惧，传播无必要的作案细节也有风险。
  • 保留报道角度：公众需要了解安全问题、司法处理和监管失误，全面沉默会减少监督。
  • 替代做法：核实信息、减少猎奇细节、重视预防和司法结果；说明这为何优于全面停止。

本题审题与一种计划

    大体不同意停止报道。保留公共预警和后续追踪，删减没有新闻价值的暴力画面及可辨认受害人的细节。

    • 支持限制 → 重复播放袭击画面和放大受害人特征 → 观众或能辨认当事人，家属也更容易反复接触画面 → 省略这些细节仍能告知事件概要。
    • 反对全面停止报道 → 居民收不到局部风险的可靠消息，后续也较难了解机构如何处置 → 核实事件、提供安全信息并追踪处置，可以保留报道的公共价值。
    • 换题时调整立场 → 如果题目只要求限制过度详细的描述，可以改为大体同意，同时说明哪些必要事实仍应公开。

    易错点

    • 圈出 stop broadcasting 与 restrict details。前者问整类报道是否应停，后者问报道里哪些内容应受限。举例写新闻报道，紧扣暴力犯罪和媒体的处理方式。
    完整原创范文

    Violent crimes can be reported in ways that are distressing to victims and their families. Nevertheless, I largely disagree with the proposal that news outlets should stop covering these incidents altogether. Accurate reports serve a public purpose, and editors can fulfil that purpose without broadcasting graphic material.

    First, timely reporting helps people respond to risks in their area. If several passengers are attacked near a railway station at night, a verified report can tell other passengers which entrance is closed and where they can find staff. It can also provide a way for witnesses to contact the police. Later coverage can examine whether the station operator repaired broken lighting or improved staffing. A ban would remove a channel for both immediate warnings and follow-up reporting, leaving people more dependent on rumours and isolated social media posts.

    At the same time, there are good reasons to limit how incidents are shown. Repeated footage of an assault may expose a victim's identity and make the incident harder for the family to escape. Showing an injured person in close-up adds little if the report can explain what happened using a map and a brief, factual account. In the railway example, the broadcaster can give the location, the time and advice for travellers while leaving out footage of the victim. A later report can focus on whether promised safety measures have been carried out.

    In conclusion, reporting violent crime is justified when it provides verified information that people can use or helps them scrutinise an institution's response. Graphic footage and identifying details should be included only when they are necessary for that purpose.
