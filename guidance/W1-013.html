完整原创范文

The diagrams show how the south wing of Walton Museum changed between 2008 and 2012 as a result of redevelopment.

Overall, the wing gained more space above the original rooms, along with a restaurant and several other visitor facilities. Access between levels was improved by the addition of a lift, while the special exhibition room and the shop retained their positions at the bottom of the building.

On the left, the entrance originally led in at the level above the special exhibition room. By 2012, an entrance hall occupied this area and a terrace had been added immediately above it. In the middle of the building, the reception stayed above the shop but was combined with the cloakroom. A restaurant was built on the next level, directly over the combined reception and cloakroom.

On the right, the staircase that previously stood between the shop and the cloakroom gave way to a lift serving several levels. The children's play area took the place of the former cloakroom, below the reading room. Above the reading room, a self-service room and a further exhibition room occupied the two upper levels.
