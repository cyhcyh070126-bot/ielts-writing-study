展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

Overall, the share of older people is expected to rise in all three countries. Aster is projected to remain the oldest by this measure, but Cedar is forecast to record the largest increase and overtake Beryl.

  • 总览写三地均预计上升，Aster 保持最高，Cedar 增幅最大并超过 Beryl。先对比 2000 年的实际值，再写 2050 年的预测值。

易错点

  • 预测值用 is projected to 或 is expected to。增幅 19 个百分点可以写 19 percentage points，19% 是另一种计算。
句框 · 按内容填槽

可用句框

[Place] is projected to record the largest increase, from [x]% in [past year] to [y]% by [forecast year].

完整原创范文

The bar chart shows the proportions of residents aged 60 or above in Aster, Beryl and Cedar in 2000, alongside the corresponding projections for 2050.

Overall, the share of older people is expected to rise in all three countries. Aster is projected to remain the oldest by this measure, but Cedar is forecast to record the largest increase and overtake Beryl.

In 2000, Aster had the highest proportion of people in this age group, at 18%. This was six percentage points above Beryl, where the figure was 12%. Cedar had the lowest share, at just 8%, ten percentage points below Aster.

By 2050, the proportion in Aster is forecast to reach 32%, an increase of 14 percentage points. Beryl is expected to rise by a slightly smaller amount, to 25%, while Cedar’s share is projected to climb by 19 points to 27%. As a result, the difference between the highest and lowest proportions is forecast to narrow from ten points in 2000 to seven in 2050.
