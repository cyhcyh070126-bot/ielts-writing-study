完整原创范文

The diagram illustrates an invented system for growing pineapples and preparing three products: fresh fruit in boxes, bottled juice and packaged dried fruit.

Overall, all three products share a cultivation and handling sequence before the fruit is divided among separate routes. One route keeps the pineapples whole, while the other two involve peeling and different forms of processing.

The common sequence begins with planting young plants, which are allowed to grow to maturity. The fruit is then harvested, washed and sorted. Only after these stages does the diagram divide into its three product lines. Pineapples intended for sale as fresh fruit are packed whole in boxes, with no peeling or further processing stage shown on that branch.

For juice production, the pineapples are peeled and chopped before being pressed. The resulting juice is then bottled. On the third route, the fruit is peeled and sliced, after which the slices are dried and packaged. The key contrast is therefore between whole-fruit packing, liquid extraction and drying. Both processed products require peeling, but only the juice branch uses pressing and only the dried-fruit route uses drying.
