    句框 · 按内容填槽

    可用句框

    The main cost falls on [affected group], particularly when [price condition].

    可用句框

    Even so, the benefit is greater if the revenue is used for [a service affected by demand], since [reason it offsets the cost].

    完整原创范文

    An overnight visitor fee can help a city pay for the services that tourists use. Such a charge adds to the price of a trip and may affect some businesses, but I believe its advantages are greater when the fee is modest and its proceeds are used to meet costs created by visitors.

    The main disadvantage is that even a small fixed charge represents a larger share of a cheap room rate than of an expensive one. A person comparing beds in two nearby towns might choose the town without a fee, especially when travelling with a group for several nights. This matters to hostels and other businesses that compete mainly on price. A charge revealed only at the end of a booking can also frustrate customers, who may have based their choice on a price that excluded it. Publishing the full price in advance and keeping the charge low can limit these effects.

    The stronger argument for the fee is that tourists add to demand for public services without necessarily contributing directly to their maintenance. A busy district may need more frequent waste collection and longer opening hours for public toilets during holiday periods. Without a separate source of revenue, the city might either reduce the standard of these services or cover the additional cost from its general budget. A modest charge spread across many overnight stays can support the extra work while adding relatively little to each stay. Visitors also benefit when the streets and facilities they use are kept in good condition.

    On balance, the fee is worthwhile if it is low, clearly stated and used for services that face extra demand. Under those conditions, the improvements to the city justify the additional cost to most visitors.
