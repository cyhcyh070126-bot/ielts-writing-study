展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

饼图抓本土出生的显著优势，表格抓五组均以城市居住为主，再指出中国出生组最集中、新西兰出生组的农村占比最高。

  • 引言同时改写两张图。The chart shows how [population] is distributed by [grouping variable], while the table compares [outcome] within [groups].
  • 总览一句给主导类别，一句给共同点和极值。Overall, [category] accounted for the largest share, and [pattern] applied to every group shown in the table.
  • 主体一按饼图由大到小分组。先写 Australia 与 Other，再把 UK 和其余三个具名海外出生地放在一起比较。
  • 主体二按表格城市比例分三组。先写 China 的极值，再并写 Netherlands 与 UK，最后对比 Australia 和 New Zealand。

易错点

  • 73% 的分母是澳大利亚全体居民，83% 的分母是在澳大利亚出生的居民。写出生地用 people born in Australia 或 Australian-born residents，保留居住地和出生地的区别。
完整原创范文

The pie chart breaks down Australia's resident population by birthplace, while the table shows the proportions of five groups who live in cities and rural areas within Australia.

Overall, people born in Australia made up by far the largest share of residents. City living predominated in every birthplace group in the table, with the highest urban share among residents born in China and the lowest among those born in New Zealand.

Australian-born residents represented 73% of the total, leaving 27% who were born overseas. The category for other birthplaces accounted for 14%, twice the share born in the United Kingdom. The other three named overseas birthplaces together represented only 6%, with New Zealand accounting for 3%, China for 2% and the Netherlands for 1%.

Turning to the table, 99% of residents born in China lived in cities. The corresponding figures for the Netherlands and the United Kingdom were close, at 90% and 89%, respectively. The urban proportions were lower for those born in Australia and New Zealand, at 83% and 80%. These two groups consequently had the highest rural shares, at 17% and 20%, respectively.
