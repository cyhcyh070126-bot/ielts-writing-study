展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

四国总量均上升。美国总量两年都居首，2006 年每千人比率最低。新西兰总量两年都最小，每千人比率反而最高。

  • 引言交代两套分母。The table compares [absolute totals] in [years] and the corresponding [rates] in [year].
  • 总览写共同趋势与排名反转。Overall, all four totals increased, and the countries with the largest and smallest totals showed the reverse pattern on a per-capita basis.
  • 主体一比较两个年份的总人数，写美国领先、英国增幅和超过澳大利亚。用 overtook 和 increased by 表达变化。
  • 主体二按每千人比率排序。用 per 1,000 residents 写单位，再回扣总人数排名反转。

易错点

  • 2.3 是每千名居民中的人数。描述英国变化可写 rose by 129,000 to 266,000。表格记录在某年统计的劳工人数，动词用 had 或 recorded。
完整原创范文

The table gives the number of temporary migrant workers in Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom and the United States in 2003 and 2006, together with the rate per thousand residents in the second year.

Overall, all four countries recorded increases. The United States had the largest workforce of this kind in both years but the lowest rate in 2006. New Zealand showed the opposite pattern, with the smallest totals and the highest rate.

In 2003, the United States recorded 577,000 workers, well ahead of Australia and the United Kingdom, at 152,000 and 137,000 respectively. By 2006, the US total had risen to 678,000. The UK recorded the largest numerical increase, adding 129,000 to reach 266,000 and overtaking Australia, whose total rose to 219,000. New Zealand also saw an increase, from 65,000 to 87,000.

The ranking was different once population size was taken into account. New Zealand had 21.1 workers per thousand residents in 2006, almost twice Australia's rate of 10.7. The United Kingdom and the United States recorded considerably lower figures, at 4.4 and 2.3 per thousand respectively.
