展开审题、观点与写法

概述与分组

北部农业和林地逐步转向开发用途；2030 规划继续扩建住宅并新增商业用途。主路、车站和西南住房全程保留。

  • 细节一写 2010→2020 已发生的变化；细节二写 2030 规划，再点明不变设施。
  • 2010、2020 用过去时；2030 用 is proposed／is planned／would 等规划表达，不能混成全部已建成。

易错点

  • 2020 仍保留一部分森林；完全消失只出现在 2030 规划图。
  • 不是每张地图都各写一段：概述后用已发生变化／规划变化分组更紧凑。
  • business park 指商务园区；sports field 是运动场，不要混成普通公园。
句框 · 按内容填槽

引言

The maps show changes to [place] between [year] and [year], alongside a proposed layout for [future year].

开头就交代第三图是规划。

概述

Overall, the district has moved from [initial land use] towards [new uses], and further development is planned. [Features] remain unchanged across the three layouts.

描述土地用途变化，不把商业化等同于经济增长。

细节一

Between [past years], [A] was replaced by [B]. Meanwhile, part of [C] was cleared for [D], although [remaining feature] was retained.

part of 与全部清除的范围要区分。

细节二

Under the [future year] plan, [A] would become [B], and [C] would be extended. [D] is also proposed for the site of [E].

would 表示规划结果；另补重要保留项。

完整原创范文

The three maps illustrate Riverside district in 2010 and 2020 and show the proposed arrangement of the same area in 2030, with a main road dividing its northern and southern sections.

Overall, agricultural and wooded land in the north gave way partly to development, and further building is planned. The main road, station and southwestern housing remain unchanged across the layouts, while the other sites undergo changes in use.

In 2010, farmland occupied the northwest and forest covered the northeast. By 2020, an industrial estate had replaced the farmland. New housing had also been constructed on the western part of the forest, leaving a smaller wooded area beside the eastern boundary. South of the main road, the housing, sports field and station were retained.

The 2030 proposal would replace the industrial estate with a business park. Housing in the northeast would expand eastwards to occupy the remaining forest. In addition, a shopping centre is planned for the southeastern site currently occupied by the sports field. No changes are proposed to the road or to the housing and station in the southwest.
